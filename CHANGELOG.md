## [0.1.0](https://github.com/casmith/ps2hdd/releases/tag/v0.1.0) (2026-08-30)

First tagged release. Reads a PS2 APA disk natively, installs PS2 and PS1
titles, fetches artwork, and ships a TUI alongside the CLI.
